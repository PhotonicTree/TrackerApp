Marker centre annotation

Each picture is a small, enlarged piece of skin with a drawn marker on it. Click the centre of the marker as exactly as you can. Take the centre of the whole dark dot, not of its darkest pixel.

  • The marker is not in the middle of the picture on purpose. The dashed circle shows where to look: if more than one marker is visible, mark the one inside or nearest to the circle.
  • You can click again to correct, or move the mark with the arrow keys (Shift for larger steps). Enter or Space confirms and moves on.
  • If there is no marker, or you cannot tell where its centre is (blurred, hidden by a fold, cut by the edge), press N: not visible.
  • B goes back, C switches contrast enhancement on and off.
  • Work at your own pace; progress is saved in this browser after every picture, so you can close the page and continue later with the same name. Some pictures appear twice; that is intended.
  • When you are done, press Export and send the downloaded file back.
